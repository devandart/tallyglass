    Why do I have to pick the folder?
    • Browsers never let a web page open a folder on your computer by itself. You choose it once; Chrome and Edge then remember it, so later scans are one click.
    • Picking the whole .claude folder works too; only projects is read.
    • Some browsers say “upload” when you pick a folder. Nothing is sent anywhere: the files are read by this page, locally.
